Civilization VII mods by genginsbon

A hand-drawn map of Europe, the Mediterranean and North Africa with historical start locations, and three civilizations to play on it: Byzantium, the Etruscans and Tuscany. Everything is free and open on GitHub.

The map

Map4 map types12 languages

Europe, Mediterranean, North Africa

Urals to Iceland, Morocco to the Sinai, and on the large maps down to the Sahel, Ethiopia and Arabia. Real coastlines, seas, rivers and mountain ranges projected onto the hex grid, with Rome, Egypt, Persia, Carthage, the Normans and twenty other civilizations starting where they did in history.

Four map types: Europe & Mediterranean with Africa, Scandinavia and Iceland as Distant Lands or as one landmass, and Eurasia Compressed - Russia turned into an Eastern Ocean with China, Korea, Mongolia and Japan beyond it - with or without Distant Lands. Three grid sizes up to 144x126.

The civilizations

Each civilization installs on its own and works on any map. With the map mod installed they start at their historical city.

Byzantium

Exploration AgeTime-Tested at any age

The Queen of Cities: Great Works feed Influence and Gold, fortified districts hold, and Hagia Sophia rises faster. Reached from Rome and Greece; leads on to Russia and the Ottomans.

  • Cataphract cavalry line and the Greek-fire Dromon
  • Augustaion quarter: Hippodrome and Great Palace
  • Hagia Sophia wonder with its own art
  • Antiquity kit: Clibanarii, Liburna, Cistern and Milion

Starts at Constantinople.

Etruscans

Antiquity AgeLeader: Lars Porsenna

Twelve peoples between the Tiber and the Arno, engineers and banqueters. Disciplina Etrusca gives Production and Happiness everywhere and speeds up buildings and the Fanum Voltumnae.

  • Biga chariot and the Tyrrhenian Galley
  • Spura quarter: Cuniculus and Tumulus
  • Fanum Voltumnae, the League's sanctuary
  • Porsenna of Clusium, militaristic and scientific

Starts at Populonia; leads to the Normans and to Tuscany.

Tuscany

Exploration AgeLeader: Lorenzo il Magnifico

Bankers, navigators and painters. Rinascimento pays Gold and Culture in every settlement and per Great Work, and the Piazza unlocks the Maestro, a unique Great Person class.

  • Ten Maestri: Leonardo, Raffaello, Michelangelo, Botticelli, Donatello, Brunelleschi, Dante, Machiavelli, Galileo, Vespucci
  • Condottiero infantry and the Galea di Santo Stefano
  • Piazza quarter: Bottega and Banco
  • Santa Maria del Fiore, Brunelleschi's dome

Starts at Florence; leads to America and the French Empire.

How to install

  1. Get the files: git clone https://github.com/marcobonechi/civ7mods.git, or use Code → Download ZIP on GitHub and unpack it.
  2. Copy the mod folders you want (EuropeMediterranean, Byzantium, Etruscans, Tuscany) into the game's Mods folder: 
    Windows:  %LOCALAPPDATA%\Firaxis Games\Sid Meier's Civilization VII\Mods
macOS:    ~/Library/Application Support/Civilization VII/Mods
  3. Restart the game. It reads mods only at startup and enables new ones automatically.

The repository also contains a browser-based geography editor with live preview and the tooling used to build the icons, art packages and screenshots. Issues and ideas are welcome on GitHub.
